A little about me

Engineering ideas into the real world.

Originally from Northamptonshire, I moved to Manchester in 2018 to study Mechatronic Engineering at The University of Manchester. During my industrial placement, I worked as a robotics engineer at WAAM3D, developing experience in automation, PLC development, industrial robotics, welding and additive manufacturing. I graduated with honours in 2023.

I am now a postgraduate researcher in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, working within the Centre for Robotic Autonomy in Demanding and Long-lasting Environments (CRADLE). Alongside research, I enjoy outreach and volunteering, including helping introduce robotics to Year 6–8 students across the Manchester area.

Current work

Research interests

Bringing together robot motion, 3D perception and practical inspection needs to help robots work reliably in complex spaces.

01 / INSPECTION

Radiological surveying

Developing a collaborative robot (cobot) radiological survey assistant to support contamination monitoring and identify accessible scanning regions on 3D objects.

02 / PLANNING

Coverage path planning

Planning collision-aware paths that maximise surface coverage while accounting for probe geometry, object shape and real-world constraints.

03 / AUTONOMY

Mobile manipulation

Exploring planning and control approaches for mobile manipulators inspecting confined and clustered environments, including nuclear facilities.
